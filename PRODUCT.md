# NexusMind product records

NexusMind is an open-source AI control plane for shared memory, code context,
SDD, tasks, harnesses, agents, policy, audit and administration.

Product and visual decisions are scoped to their surfaces:

- [Admin](apps/admin/PRODUCT.md): organization operations and Factory workflows.
- [Landing](apps/landing/PRODUCT.md): the approved marketing surface and factual
  capability descriptions; no waitlist or invented customer metrics.
- Backoffice: internal, superadmin-only operations across organizations.

[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) links the current visual records. The historical blue/glass
system from PR #271 does not override the later application-specific decisions.
The bootcamp product and Rust TUIs remain outside this web design scope.
